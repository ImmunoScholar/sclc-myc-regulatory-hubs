!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = ChIP-seq on cell lines (H1836, SHP-77)  were performed using 1x107 cells. Samples were crosslinked at room temperature for 10 min by swirling with 1% formaldehyde (Sigma Aldrich) followed by addition of 125 mM glycine to quench the reaction. After washing the cells with PBS, cells were resuspended with ChIP lysis buffer supplemented with protease inhibitors (Roche cOmplete mini EDTA-free protease inhibitor tablets, Cat #: 11836170001) and phosphatase inhibitors (Thermo Scientific, Halt Phosphatase Inhibitor Cocktail, Cat #: 78427, 1:1000). Cell lysates in 1 mL ChIP lysis buffer were sonicated to shear chromatin to 200-500 bp. Supernatants were incubated with the antibodies (IgG: Cell Signaling Technology, Cat #: 2729S and ASCL1: Cell Signaling Technology, Cat #: 43666S) at 4ºC overnight on a rotator. Dynabeads™ Protein G were blocked with 2.5 mg/mL UltraPure™ BSA (Thermo Scientific, Cat #: AM2616) at 4ºC overnight. The beads were washed three times with ChIP buffer (50 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0, 150 mM NaCl, 1mM EDTA, 1% Triton X-100) and incubated with the chromatin-antibody samples. These samples were washed with high salt ChIP buffer (20 mM Tris, pH 8.0, 500 mM NaCl, 2 mM EDTA, 1% of Triton X-100, 0.1% of SDS) followed by washings with Tris-EDTA (TE) buffer. Chromatin complexes were eluted in TE with 1% ultrapure SDS (ThermoFisher Scientific) at 65ºC for 15 min, and reverse-crosslinked with RNAse A (ThermoFisher Scientific, Cat #: EN0531), followed by incubation with Proteinase K solution (ThermoFisher Scientific, Cat #: EO0491). DNA was purified using QIAquick PCR Purification Kit (Qiagen).
